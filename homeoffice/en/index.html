Frequently asked questions

What is HomeOffice?

It is an iPhone and iPad app for hybrid workers who must meet a minimum share of days at the office. You log each day as office, home, business trip, vacation, sick, time off or closure, and the app works out whether you meet your monthly target and, if your company has one, your performance bonus target.

How is the percentage calculated?

It starts from the workdays in the period (Monday–Friday or Monday–Saturday), removes public holidays, vacation, sick days, time off and closures, then applies the required percentage. The result is rounded the way your company does it: for example 22 days at 40% is 8.8, which becomes 8 when rounding down or 9 when rounding up. Business trips count as office days.

Do vacation, sick days and holidays count?

No, they do not lower your percentage: they are removed from the workdays the target is based on. Public holidays for 15 European countries are built in; local holidays and company closures can be added in Settings.

What is the performance bonus in the app?

It is an optional second target with a different percentage (for example 35%) measured over a working year whose start and end you choose. The app shows how many days you have done, how many you need, how many days a week are still required and, if it is out of reach, the highest percentage you can still achieve.

Can I log half days?

Yes. Each day has a morning and an afternoon: for example morning at the office and afternoon at home, or half a day of vacation. Each half counts as 0.5 days in the calculation.

Where does my data go?

It stays in your iPhone's storage and is synced to your personal iCloud account, so you find it on your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. There are no developer servers and the app contains no ads. It only collects anonymous usage statistics, which do not include your days.

Can I import my attendance from Excel?

Yes. The app reads an .xlsx file with a Date column and a day type column, or with Morning and Afternoon columns. It recognises common labels in five languages, such as Office, Remote, Vacation or Business trip, and you can export everything to Excel and PDF.

Which devices and languages are supported?

It works on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. The interface is available in English, Italian, French, German and Spanish, and the app language can be chosen independently of the system language.
