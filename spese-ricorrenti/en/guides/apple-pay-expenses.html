How to log your Apple Pay payments as expenses

People stop tracking expenses almost always for the same reason: logging them afterwards is a chore. But if you pay with your phone, the payment already leaves a trace: the Wallet notification. You can use that.

The problem with everyday payments

A coffee, the groceries, gas: small amounts, many times a week. Writing them down by hand at the end of the day means forgetting half of them. It works much better when they're logged the moment you pay.

Four ways to log them, from the most automatic

  • Automation: you set it up once in Shortcuts (Apple's app). From then on every Apple Pay payment lands in your expenses, with merchant, amount and date, without opening the app.
  • Sharing the notification: if you prefer to decide yourself, share the payment notification with Skurda from iOS's Share sheet, with a tap.
  • Receipt photo: useful for cash or other cards. Merchant, amount and date are recognised from the text and the photo is never saved.
  • By hand: a few seconds, always available.

What you should know

  • It's not a bank connection or an official Apple Pay integration: Skurda only reads the Wallet notification on your own phone and never asks for banking credentials.
  • If the notification is missing some detail, you can complete the expense by hand afterwards.
  • Your first 5 automatically logged payments are free, to try it. To keep going without limits (automatically or from the notification) you need Premium; by hand and from a receipt you get 20 daily expenses a month for free.
  • Daily expenses always stay separate from recurring ones, and are never quietly added together.

How to get started

  1. Download Skurda: it's free and needs no account.
  2. Follow the automation guide inside the app: you do it once and it takes a few minutes.
  3. Pay with your phone as usual.
  4. Open Home: your daily expenses are already there.

Skurda is free for up to 10 recurring and 20 daily expenses a month, no account. Your data stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud.
