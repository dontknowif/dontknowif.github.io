How to log your Apple Pay payments as expenses

People stop tracking expenses almost always for the same reason: logging them afterwards is a chore. But if you pay with your phone, the payment already leaves a trace: the Wallet notification. You can use that.

Updated 21 November 2026

The problem with everyday payments

A coffee, the groceries, gas: small amounts, many times a week. Writing them down by hand at the end of the day means forgetting half of them. It works much better when they're logged the moment you pay.

Four ways to log them, from the most automatic

  • Automation: you set it up once in Shortcuts (Apple's app). From then on every Apple Pay payment lands in your expenses, with merchant, amount and date, without opening the app.
  • Sharing the notification: if you prefer to decide yourself, share the payment notification with Skurda from iOS's Share sheet, with a tap.
  • Receipt photo: useful for cash or other cards. Merchant, amount and date are recognised from the text and the photo is never saved.
  • By hand: a few seconds, always available.

How to set up the automation, step by step

You do it once, in a few minutes, in the Shortcuts app that's already on your iPhone:

  1. Open Shortcuts, go to the Automation tab and tap +.
  2. Pick Wallet as the trigger, leave all cards selected and choose Run Immediately.
  3. Create a new shortcut, search for Skurda and add the Record payment action.
  4. In the action's two fields, pick the transaction's Amount and Merchant, then confirm.

From then on, every contactless payment with a Wallet card reaches Skurda on its own. The full guide with pictures and a video shows every screen; the same guide is inside the app, and on iOS 27 there's a ready-made shortcut you can add in one tap.

What gets read, and what doesn't

From the payment notification Skurda takes only three things: merchant, amount and date. It doesn't read your balance, card number or account activity, and it has no access to your bank. Everything happens on the iPhone: expenses are saved on the phone and in your iCloud, never on our servers. If a notification is missing the amount, the expense is saved anyway and you can complete it later.

The first time you pay at a shop you choose the category; from then on Skurda remembers it for that merchant.

What you should know

  • It's not a bank connection or an official Apple Pay integration: Skurda only reads the Wallet notification on your own phone and never asks for banking credentials.
  • If the notification is missing some detail, you can complete the expense by hand afterwards.
  • Your first 5 automatically logged payments are free, to try it. To keep going without limits (automatically or from the notification) you need Premium; by hand and from a receipt you get 20 daily expenses a month for free.
  • Daily expenses always stay separate from recurring ones, and are never quietly added together.

How to get started

  1. Download Skurda: it's free and needs no account.
  2. Follow the automation guide inside the app: you do it once and it takes a few minutes.
  3. Pay with your phone as usual.
  4. Open Home: your daily expenses are already there.

Skurda is free for up to 10 recurring and 20 daily expenses a month, no account. Your data stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud.

Frequently asked questions

Can Skurda see my bank account or card?

No. It only reads the merchant, amount and date from the payment notification, on your own iPhone. It never asks for banking credentials, can't see your balance and has no access to your card.

Do I have to open the app every time I pay?

No. With the Shortcuts automation the payment is logged in the background, without opening Skurda. If you turn on the automation's notifications, you get a note with the result, and tapping it opens the expense just saved.

What if I pay a bill I already track as a recurring expense?

If the payment matches a recurring expense you already have, Skurda marks it as paid instead of creating a new expense, so it isn't counted twice.

What about payments with a physical card or in cash?

The automation works with cards saved in Wallet when you pay with your iPhone. For everything else there's the receipt photo and the manual form, which takes a few seconds.

Can I try it without paying?

Yes. The first 5 automatically logged payments are free, so you can see how it works before deciding. From the sixth one you need Premium; logging by hand and from receipts stays free up to 20 expenses a month.
