How to track your subscriptions

One €9.99 subscription is no big deal. Ten of them add up to over a thousand euros a year, and you only remember many of them when the charge appears. Here is a five-step method to always know what you pay, when and why.

1. List everything

Go through the last three months of bank statements, your receipt emails and, on iPhone, the subscriptions list in your Apple Account settings. Include the yearly ones: they are the easiest to forget.

2. Note four things for each

Name, amount, how often it renews and the date of the next charge. Add where to cancel too: when you decide to stop, you don't want to waste time looking for it.

3. Bring everything to the same period

A €120-a-year subscription costs €10 a month; one at €4.99 a month costs almost €60 a year. Converting everything into a monthly and a yearly total makes comparisons immediate.

4. Get warned before the charge

The right time to decide is before the renewal goes through, especially for yearly renewals and free trials that turn into paid ones. Set a reminder a few days before each due date.

5. Review every three or four months

Look for streaming services active at the same time (often one at a time, in rotation, is enough), price increases that went unnoticed, subscriptions you no longer use and duplicates.
