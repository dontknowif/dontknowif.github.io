Your whole care plan, in order

Made for people with recurring visits, tests and therapies who want to stop keeping it all in their head.

One plan for each condition

Several care plans, each with its own appointments, specialists and documents. There is one family doctor, shared by all of them.

Appointments that repeat

Visits, tests, medicine pick-ups, therapies: every so many days, weeks, months or years, with a time if needed. Mark it done and the app sets the next one.

Things to do beforehand

For each appointment, what needs doing in advance: asking for a referral, booking, picking up, planning the trip. Each with its own lead time.

Reminders that insist

A notification at the time you choose. If you don't mark something as done, the reminder comes back every few days until the appointment.

Ready-made templates

Hospital medicine, blood tests, check-up visit, yearly scan, therapy plan renewal, vaccine, exemption, periodic therapy: pick and adapt.

Emails to your doctor, already written

The referral request with the list of tests, to one or more recipients, signed with your name. You read it over and send it from your own mailbox.

Doctors at hand

Family doctor and specialists with phone, email and office hours. Call or write with one tap.

Documents always with you

Reports, prescriptions and therapy plans as PDFs or photos, also from Share in any app. Attach them to an email when needed.

Agenda, widgets and Calendar

“Upcoming” shows what is overdue, what is on this week, within a month and later on. Widgets for the Home and Lock Screen, and Add to Calendar.

Your health data stays yours

Visits, tests and therapies are among the most personal information there is. That is why Piano di Cura is built this way.

None of our servers

Everything stays on your iPhone and, if you want, in your personal iCloud, directly between you and Apple. We see nothing.

No ads, no tracking

The app contains no ad networks and does not track you. It only collects anonymous usage statistics, never health data.

No account

No sign-up needed. Your name, if you enter it, is only used to sign emails to your doctors.

Emails are sent by you

The app prepares the text, you read it over and send it from your own mailbox. No copy goes through us.

All the details are in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

What is Piano di Cura?

It is an iPhone app for people following a course of care over time: check-up visits, blood tests, hospital medicine pick-ups, periodic therapies, renewals of therapy plans and exemptions. For each appointment you list the things to do beforehand, such as asking for a referral or booking, and the app reminds you of each one at the right time.

When does it come out?

Piano di Cura is coming to the App Store by the end of 2026, for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. If you would like a heads-up on launch day, write to us at feedbacksr@icloud.com.

Can I follow more than one condition?

Yes. You can create several care plans, for example one for each condition, each with its own appointments, specialists and documents. The “Upcoming” agenda shows them all together or one at a time.

How do reminders work?

Each thing to do has its own lead time, for example booking a blood draw a month before the tests. You get a notification at the time you choose and, if you don't mark it as done, the reminder comes back every few days until the appointment. You can choose how often, or turn it off.

Can I write to my doctor from the app?

Yes. The app prepares the email asking for a referral or prescription, with the list of tests and your name, to one or more recipients. The email opens in your email app: you read it over, edit it if you like and send it yourself.

Where does my data go?

It stays in your iPhone's storage and, if you want, it is synced to your personal iCloud account, so you find it on your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. There is no developer server and the app contains no ads. It only collects anonymous usage statistics, which never contain health data.

Does Piano di Cura replace my doctor?

No. Piano di Cura is a personal agenda: it helps you remember appointments and things to do, but it gives no medical advice, is not a medical device and does not replace the advice of your doctor or pharmacist.

Which languages is it available in?

The app is available in English, Italian, Spanish, German and French, and follows the language set on your iPhone. Dates and times use your country's format.
